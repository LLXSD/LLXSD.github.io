I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Network Science and Cyberspace, Tsinghua University, in 2026, advised by [Prof. Yuanjie Li](https://www.yuanjiel.com/). Prior to that, I obtained my Master’s degree in Cyberspace Security from Tsinghua University in June 2022 (advised by Prof. Hewu Li) and my Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Shandong University in June 2019. My research interests include satellite networking and mobile networks. My works are published at conferences and journals such as USENIX NSDI, ACM SIGCOMM, ACM MobiCom, IEEE INFOCOM, IEEE IWQoS, and ACM HotNets.
